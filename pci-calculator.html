How PCI is calculated

An interactive walkthrough — add distresses to a road image and watch the PCI score update in real time. Based on ASTM D6433, adapted for vialytics' image-based detection.

Step 1 Distress detection

vialytics' AI analyzes the lower part of each forward-facing road image (a 13-foot segment). Detections are marked as bounding boxes, except longitudinal/transverse and alligator cracks which are returned as polygons. Click any detection to remove it.

Click any detection to remove it. Bounding boxes for block, patching, potholes & raveling. Polygons for longitudinal/transverse and alligator cracks.

Step 2 Distress density (%)

For each PCI category, density = total detected damage area ÷ analyzed road surface area. Click [+ Add] to drop one detection on the road.

Step 3 Deduct value (DV) per category

Each density % maps to a Deduct Value via ASTM D6433-like curves. Higher-severity distresses (potholes, alligator cracks) deduct more even at low density. Curves below are illustrative approximations.

Step 4 Maximum CDV → PCI

When multiple distresses are present, raw deduct values would over-count. ASTM defines a Corrected Deduct Value (CDV) algorithm that combines them with diminishing returns based on q (count of significant DVs > 2). Final PCI = 100 − maxCDV.
